About Our Studio

Designing Spaces. Creating Experiences.

Founded on the belief that environment deeply governs well-being, Atelier Aurelia unites interior architecture, custom furniture curation, and atmospheric lighting into unified works of living art.

From sprawling coastal villas and historic brownstone restorations to boutique culinary sanctuaries, we collaborate with discerning clients worldwide. Our bespoke approach pairs sustainable natural materials with contemporary ergonomic design.

Holistic Space Planning

Fluid circulation, generous natural lighting, and intelligent spatial balance.

Methodology

The Four-Stage Design Journey

A seamless, transparent collaboration taking your space from abstract ambition to breathtaking tangible reality.

01

1. Discover

We commence with an in-depth creative discovery: understanding your daily rituals, aesthetic desires, architectural site conditions, and budget objectives.

02

2. Plan

Spatial optimization, architectural CAD layouts, lighting schemes, and initial physical material sample trays (wood, marble, fabrics) for tactile alignment.

03

3. Design

Photorealistic 3D visualizations, custom joinery blueprints, trade procurement lists, and comprehensive scheduling down to the finest detail.

04

4. Transform

On-site artisan supervision, precision installation, turnkey furniture placement, and final white-glove styling ready for your first unforgettable evening.
