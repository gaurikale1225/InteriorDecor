Our Interior Disciplines

Tailored architectural and styling services engineered to transform empty volumes into deeply atmospheric living sanctuaries.

Discipline 01

Residential Interior Architecture

End-to-end turnkey architectural transformations for luxury villas, penthouses, and suburban estates.

We choreograph every square foot: reconfiguring partition walls, designing custom architectural millwork, selecting acoustic materials, and integrating concealed HVAC and home automation systems seamlessly into the architecture.

  • Full architectural floorplans & RCP (Reflected Ceiling Plans)
  • Custom built-in cabinetry, bookcases, and fire hearths
  • Turnkey on-site contractor management and milestone oversight
Inquire Residential Project
Discipline 02

Living Room & Salon Design

Fostering natural conversation and relaxed elegance through curated seating clusters and ambient hearths.

We craft living spaces that balance intimate evening relaxation with expansive entertaining capacity. Incorporating oversized linen sofas, travertine cocktail tables, textured wool rugs, and sculptural acoustic wall treatments.

  • Bespoke modular sofas and Italian leather lounge chairs
  • Architectural fireplace surrounds in carved limestone or patinated metal
  • Integrated discreet media walls with acoustic dampening
Inquire Living Room Design
Discipline 03

Bedroom Sanctuary Design

Serene sleeping chambers designed for deep restorative rest and multisensory tactile tranquility.

Prioritizing circadian health through soft diffused indirect lighting, blackout motorized drapery, natural mineral paints, and custom acoustic upholstered headboards crafted in raw bouclé and linen.

  • Custom floating oak platform beds and integrated nightstands
  • Walk-in dressing rooms with illuminated smoked-glass wardrobe joinery
  • En-suite master bath spatial transitions with fluted privacy glass
Inquire Bedroom Sanctuary
Discipline 04

Modular Kitchen Architecture

Architectural culinary sanctuaries combining European engineering precision with artisanal finishes.

We merge chef-grade appliances (Gaggenau, Sub-Zero, La Cornue) with handleless solid oak frontals, honed marble waterfall counters, concealed spice larders, and dedicated wine sommelier vaults.

  • Ergonomic culinary work triangles and concealed appliance pantries
  • Monolithic bookmatched Calacatta or Taj Mahal quartzite islands
  • Integrated task illumination and custom brass pot fillers
Inquire Kitchen Architecture
Discipline 05

Office & Commercial Interiors

Inspiring corporate headquarters, private wealth ataliers, and boutique retail spaces.

Translating corporate identity into physical architectural spaces that attract top-tier talent, foster collaborative ingenuity, and leave lasting impressions upon visiting clientele.

  • Executive boardrooms with acoustic clay walls and conference tables
  • Biophilic micro-zones and executive wellness lounges
  • Full ADA compliance, egress planning, and commercial fire codes

Discipline 06

Space Planning & 3D Photorealism

Experience your finished environment virtually before a single wall is built.

Using advanced BIM software and physically-based rendering (PBR), we calculate exact sunlight ingress throughout the seasons, test alternate marble slabs, and eliminate guesswork.

  • High-resolution 4K photorealistic still renderings
  • Interactive 360-degree virtual reality walkthroughs
  • Accurate millimeter-level CAD construction sets
Inquire 3D Services
Discipline 07

Renovation & Historic Remodeling

Breathing contemporary vitality into historic heritage properties and classic layouts.

Specializing in Victorian, Haussmannian, and mid-century modern restorations. We meticulously restore original crown moldings and parquet de Versailles while upgrading MEP infrastructure to 21st-century standards.

  • Historic landmark preservation committee filings and permits
  • Structural load-bearing wall removal and steel beam integration
  • Preservation of vintage plaster moldings, millwork, and brasswork
Inquire Renovation

Styling & Curated Decor Advisory

Whether you possess an existing art collection or wish to commission bespoke contemporary sculptures and hand-woven tapestries, our decor curators guide procurement with discerning taste.

  • Access to exclusive trade-only European design houses & galleries
  • Curated artwork framing, lighting, and gallery installation
  • Textile layering: cashmere blankets, linen drapery, and accent pillows
