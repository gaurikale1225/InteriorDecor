Designing Spaces. Creating Experiences.

A fourteenth-year dialogue between architecture, tactile European heritage, and quiet contemporary luxury.

Our Heritage

Fourteen Years of Sculpting Light & Materiality

Atelier Aurelia was established in 2012 by architect Aurelia Vance and designer Julian Thorne with a unified vision: to liberate interior spaces from transient trends and ground them in timeless architectural permanence.

We believe a home should act as an intentional sanctuary. By marrying honest materials—quarried Roman travertine, raw Belgian linens, patinated unlacquered brass, and solid white oak—with ergonomic precision, we design spaces that age with grace.

Today, our Manhattan and London design studios handle private estates, penthouse conversions, and boutique commercial environments across North America, Europe, and Asia.

Core Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Aurelia Design

Every architectural sketch, material board, and bespoke furniture commission is measured against our fundamental values.

Spatial Proportion

Honoring architectural rhythm, ceiling height, sightlines, and generous unencumbered circulation for effortless movement.

Tactile Materiality

Rejecting synthetic imitations in favor of raw, unlacquered, and hand-finished natural surfaces that develop authentic patina.

Atmospheric Illumination

Layering architectural shadow and circadian lighting fixtures to create intimate ambiance from dawn to late evening.

Enduring Longevity

Designing beyond transient fads to deliver heirloom-quality homes that remain inspiring and functional for generations.
